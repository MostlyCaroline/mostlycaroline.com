About me

Thoughtful design creates meaningful change.

I'm a UX & UI Product Designer based in Hampshire with 15 years of UX/UI experience and 10 years within education and teaching.

My career has been built around understanding people: how they think, what motivates them, and what helps them succeed. From supporting children with diverse learning needs to leading teams, driving business growth, and designing user-centred digital experiences, I've always been motivated by the same goal - creating environments where people can thrive.
